1. Introduction

Zalazar Holdings LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is a real estate investment and short-term luxury rental company committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, submit an inquiry, or book a stay through our properties.

By using our website or submitting your information through our contact form, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Information: First name, last name, email address, and phone number — submitted voluntarily through our contact form.
  • Booking Information: Travel dates, number of guests, property of interest, and other details you provide to plan your stay.
  • Identification (when required): For certain destinations such as Punta Cana, local properties may require a valid photo ID at check-in. This is handled at the property level and not stored on our website.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs collected automatically when you visit our website.
  • Communications: Records of your consent to receive SMS/text messages, including the date, time, and method of opt-in.

We collect information you provide directly (e.g., by filling out our contact form) and information collected automatically through cookies and similar tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide booking and reservation information for our rental properties.
  • To send you SMS/text messages you have explicitly opted into, including booking confirmations, check-in instructions, utility payment requests for applicable units, customer service responses, and occasional service updates.
  • To coordinate check-in, check-out, and on-site support during your stay.
  • To improve our website, listings, and guest experience through analytics.
  • To comply with applicable laws, tax obligations, and short-term rental regulations.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Zalazar Holdings LLC, our guests, or others.

4. SMS / Text Message Policy

⚠ SMS Consent — Important Disclosure

No mobile information (including phone numbers and SMS opt-in data) will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Opt-in consent is not transferable and is used solely to communicate with you directly about your inquiry or stay.

How you opt in: You provide express written consent to receive SMS/text messages from Zalazar Holdings LLC by checking the opt-in checkbox on our website contact form and submitting your phone number. The checkbox is unchecked by default and consent is never a condition of any purchase or booking.

Types of messages: By opting in, you may receive SMS messages including: booking confirmations, pre-arrival and check-in instructions, address and access information on the day of arrival, utility payment requests where applicable (e.g., for certain Punta Cana units), responses to your questions, and occasional service updates related to your stay.

Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on your inquiry and the timing of your stay. Most guests receive between 2 and 10 messages per booking cycle.

Message & data rates: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

How to opt out: You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent. You can opt back in at any time by replying START.

How to get help: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at info@zalazarholdings.com or +1 (888) 551-3007.

No data sharing: All categories of personal information collected in connection with SMS opt-in — including your phone number and consent records — exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. This information will not be shared with any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes under any circumstances.

Supported carriers: Most major U.S. carriers are supported. Carrier support is not guaranteed for all carriers. Zalazar Holdings LLC is not responsible for delays or failed delivery of messages caused by your mobile carrier.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who assist in operating our business or delivering our services (e.g., booking platforms such as Airbnb and VRBO, email delivery, payment processors, property managers), bound by confidentiality agreements and prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.
  • Local Property Operators: When you book a specific property, we may share check-in details with the local on-site team responsible for the unit so they can welcome you and provide support.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • Protection of Rights: When necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Zalazar Holdings LLC, our guests, or others.

We will never share your SMS opt-in data or phone number with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, manage your booking history, or as required by applicable law. SMS opt-in records and consent logs are retained for a minimum of 4 years to comply with telecommunications regulations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Opt-Out of SMS: Reply STOP to any text message at any time.
  • Do Not Sell: We do not sell your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@zalazarholdings.com or call +1 (888) 551-3007.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adult travelers (age 18 and over) booking accommodations. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Children may stay at our properties as part of a family booking made by an adult guest. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.
